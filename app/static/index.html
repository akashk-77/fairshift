🦺 FairShift

One platform, two interfaces. Workers get clarity and a voice. Employers get early warning on fatigue, safety and people who are about to quit. It replaces paper registers, spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups. (UN SDG 8: Decent Work)

👷

Worker app

For construction, factory, hospital and campus staff. Mobile first, 4 languages, voice and icons. Check in, see your payslip, report a hazard, give honest feedback, carry your skills.
📊

Employer dashboard

Switch between sectors. See attendance, fatigue alerts, quit risk, overdue safety reports, anonymous feedback and AI insights.

One product, four workplaces

SectorWho uses itHow check-in worksWhat the platform catches

FactoryLine operators, welders, packersGate QR per plantUnguarded machines, back-to-back rotating shifts, overtime at 2x
HospitalNurses, ward attendants, sanitation teamsWard QR, round-the-clock shiftsSharps and infection risk, night-shift fatigue, 11-hour rest rule
College & schoolHostel, canteen, security, lab and transport staffCampus gate QR or geofenceLab chemical hazards, fire-drill training gaps, unpaid extra hours

Each site sets its own standard hours and overtime multiplier. The same worker flow, payslip engine, fatigue rules and anonymous feedback run unchanged across all four. Only the settings and the hazard types differ, so adding a fifth sector means adding a row, not writing code.
